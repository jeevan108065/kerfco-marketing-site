When someone searches **"cnc works"**, **"jali doors"**, or **"wood works near me"**

## Part 2 — Get customer reviews (this beats your competition)

Reviews are the biggest reason one shop ranks above another. Some competitors
have 40–150 reviews. You can catch up by asking every happy customer.

### Step 1: Get your review link
1. Open your Google Business Profile
2. Tap **"Ask for reviews"**
3. Copy the short link it gives you (looks like `https://g.page/r/...../review`)
4. Save that link — you'll paste it into WhatsApp messages

### Step 2: Ask every customer (send within a day of delivery)
Copy one of these messages, replace the bracketed parts, and send on WhatsApp.

**English:**
> Hi [Name], thank you for choosing Kerfco TimberTech CNC! 🙏 We'd be grateful
> if you could share a quick Google review — it really helps our small business
> grow. It takes 30 seconds: [paste your review link]. If you can mention the
> work we did (for example: CNC jali door / 3D wall panel / pooja mandir), that
> is a big help!

**Telugu:**
> నమస్తే [Name] గారు, Kerfco TimberTech CNC ని ఎంచుకున్నందుకు ధన్యవాదాలు 🙏
> దయచేసి Google లో ఒక చిన్న review ఇవ్వగలరా? మా చిన్న business కి ఇది చాలా
> సహాయపడుతుంది. కేవలం 30 సెకన్లు: [paste your review link]. మేము చేసిన పని
> (CNC jali door / 3D wall panel / pooja mandir) గురించి ప్రస్తావిస్తే మరింత మంచిది!

### Step 3: Good review habits
- Ask the customer to **add a photo** to their review — it counts more.
- **Reply to every review**, even a simple "Thank you!" — Google likes active profiles.
- Ask **2 or 3 customers per week** rather than 20 in one day (looks more natural).
- Never pay for fake reviews — Google can detect and penalize this.

## Part 4 — Help your website show up on Google (Search Console)

This tells Google your website exists so it appears in search results.
(This is slightly technical — you can ask your web person to help, or follow along.)

### Steps
1. Open **search.google.com/search-console**
2. Sign in with the same Google account
3. Click **Add property** → choose **URL prefix** → enter:
   `https://kerfco-marketing-site.vercel.app/`
4. Verify (the easiest method shown will usually work)
5. On the left menu, click **Sitemaps**, and submit:
   `sitemap.xml`
6. Use **URL Inspection** at the top, paste your website link, and click
   **"Request indexing."**

This helps Google find and list your site faster. Results can take a few days
to a few weeks — that is normal.
