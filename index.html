Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you reach out.

What CNC and wood works do you offer in Visakhapatnam?

We do CNC cutting and routing, CNC jali sheets and jali doors, 3D wall panels and wall paneling, pooja room and mandir designs, wardrobe grooving, sliding doors, wooden partitions and room dividers, name boards and signage, and custom carpentry and furniture — all across Visakhapatnam (Vizag).

Where are you located and which areas do you serve?

We're near St. Ann's School CBSE, Bakkanapalem / Madhurawada, Visakhapatnam. We serve all of Vizag including Madhurawada, Gajuwaka, MVP Colony, Seethammadhara, Rushikonda and nearby areas.

What does a CNC project cost?

Pricing depends on the design complexity, material and size. Share a reference photo and rough dimensions on WhatsApp and we'll give you a clear quote.

How long does an order take?

Most panels and jali work are ready within a few days to a couple of weeks depending on the size and our current queue. We confirm a timeline when you place the order.

Which materials do you work with?

HDHMR, MDF, plywood and solid hardwoods like teak and neem. We'll recommend the best material for your design, budget and where it will be used.

Do you deliver and install?

Yes — we handle delivery within Visakhapatnam and can arrange installation for larger pieces. Let us know your location when you enquire.

Can you make a fully custom design?

Absolutely. Send a sketch, a Pinterest/Instagram reference or just an idea, and we'll translate it into a machine-ready design.

Is there a minimum order?

No strict minimum — we take on single decorative panels through to complete interior fit-outs. Small jobs are welcome.
